# Platform v2 — target design of the platform database (experiment 47)

Written 2026-10-20 by the lead architect, from the research brief (`research_brief.md`) and five audits of
`~/data/platform/platform.db` v46 (`audit_bibliographic.md`, `audit_people.md`, `audit_citations.md`,
`audit_content.md`, `audit_quality_scale.md`). The schema is `schema47.sql`. Its section below is generated by
`schema_doc47.py`, so the two cannot drift apart. Nothing is built yet. This is the target the v2 loader
(`load47`) will be written against. No paid calls were made.

---

## 1. The shape, in one page

Picture **one scanned page on a light table**, with clear sheets laid over it.

- **The page itself is the bottom layer, and it never changes.** It is the scan, plus Azure's reading of it: every
  word in its box, numbered in Azure's order, with Azure's confidence. Every Gemini run reads the same Azure words,
  so *"page 3, Azure words 120 to 131"* names the same piece of ink in every run, today and after any rebuild. We
  call that address an **ink anchor**. It is the pin that everything else is fastened to.
- **Each run lays a clear sheet over the page**: its reading of the article. The sheet holds paragraphs, headings,
  notes, verse lines, tables, figures, the corrections it made to Azure, and the misprints it noticed but left
  alone (D30). v33_300, v33_300_judged, v33_300_final and the exam v33_300b are four sheets over the same pages.
  They lie side by side, and none of them erases another. One sheet per document is marked **"shown"**, with the
  reason it was chosen.
- **Beside the light table is a card index of things**: publications, journals and issues, people, institutions,
  and the works that get cited, including books that Mandumah does not hold. A card is never the printed text. It is
  what we *decided* the text refers to. Every decision is a **thread from the card down to the ink**, labelled with
  who tied it (code, the judge, a person), how, and how sure they were.
- **A second drawer holds the catalogue**: Mandumah's records keyed by 999$c, exactly as each export gave them. When
  print and catalogue disagree, both stay. A **report slip** says who is right, points at the ink, and tracks
  whether Mandumah fixed it in the next export.
- **Over everything sits a quality ledger**: flags, judge looks, patches and human verdicts. Each one points at a
  pin, so any value can say *"this is how far you can trust me, and here is the ink to check."*
- **Numbers such as times cited, self-citation and coverage are never written on the cards.** They are counted
  fresh from the threads at every release.

Three rules hold the whole shape together:

1. **Printed is never overwritten.** A correction is a new reading beside the old one. This covers Azure misreadings
   (Azure's reading is kept), misprints (only proposed), metadata (D33, printed form kept) and who-is-who (patches).
   Corrections are patches ranked person > judge > gemini-retry > rule, and they are re-applied on every rebuild.
2. **A mention is not an entity.** A byline name, an affiliation line and a footnote citation are mentions. They are
   kept exactly as printed and tied to the ink. A person, an institution and a cited work are entities. A link joins
   the two and carries method, score, evidence and who asserted it.
3. **«المرجع السابق» is a mention, not a link.** «المرجع السابق», «نفسه», ibid. and op. cit. resolve straight to the
   *first full citation* of that work in the article, so chains collapse to one step. Each one counts as another
   citation of that work. `citation_links` holds only real links: from a cited work to a publication in the archive
   or the catalogue, or to an outside id.

## 2. What only we can do

None of the big databases sees footnotes in reading order, the ink behind a value, or two readings of the same
word. We have all three, plus a cataloguer-disambiguated author authority file. Every feature below is a query on
the tables named, and none needs a paid model call.

| Feature (what a user sees) | Why nobody else has it | Tables |
|---|---|---|
| **"Show me the ink"**: on any title, author, citation, verse, Quran quote, fix, flag or search hit, one click shows the crop of the page it came from | WoS, Scopus and OpenAlex hold no page geometry. PMC has no coordinates | `ink_anchors` (+ `anchor_links`), `pages.page_image_key`, `anchor_pk` on every value table |
| **A corrections ledger** for the text: Azure read X (confidence 0.41), the page says Y, and for a misprint the page says Y while Z is proposed. Searchable and reviewable | Others publish one text and no history | `text_corrections`, `reviews`, views `v_fixes`, `v_misprints` |
| **Metadata as printed and as corrected**, shown side by side with the catalogue's form, Gemini's title file and the Qdrant payload. One is chosen and the reason is given | Others keep a single resolved value | `metadata_assertions` (form, source, anchor, `is_chosen`, `chosen_rule`), `v_metadata_changes` |
| **A trust score on every value**, built from evidence (Azure confidence, fixes, flags, the judge, retries) and calibrated against human verdicts | Others have no evidence trail | `blocks.trust`, `citation_mentions.trust`, `v_block_trust`, `v_judge_precision`, `reviews.is_gold` |
| **A cited-reference index that includes books.** About 3,200 of 4,800 cited works are books that are not in Mandumah, and each still gets a times-cited | OpenAlex drops unmatched references. WoS keeps them only as variant strings | `cited_works`, `cited_work_members`, `v_times_cited` |
| **«المرجع السابق» counted right**, resolved in footnote order to the first full citation | Nobody resolves ibid. or op. cit. | `citation_mentions.full_mention_pk`, `resolve_method` |
| **A Quran citation index**: every quotation (﴾…﴿) and every reference (سورة …، الآية …), by sura and aya, across the archive | No index has it | `quran_citations`, `quran_ayat`, `v_quran_index` |
| **A verse index**: vowelled poetry searchable line by line or hemistich by hemistich, each line opening its ink | No index has it | `verse_lines`, `search_units` (kind verse) |
| **Arabic names understood**: ism, nasab, nisba, laqab and kunya, with titles («د.», «الشيخ») kept, Latin and Arabic forms tied together, and transliteration learned from the catalogue's own Latin headings | Western matchers fail on «بن …» and on Latin bylines | `person_names`, `byline_mentions.titles`, `translit_pairs`, `cited_work_authors` |
| **Authors disambiguated by cataloguers**: a Mandumah authority id counts as a "claimed profile" and gets the highest trust | WoS and Scopus rely on machine-learned clusters | `person_authorities`, `authorships.method` |
| **The academic family tree of the Arab world**: who supervised whom, where and when (245,785 supervisor rows) | No index has it | `supervisions`, `persons`, `institutions` |
| **Both calendars**: search 1397 AH or 1977 CE and find the same issue. A hijri year is never stored as a gregorian one | Others store gregorian dates only | `publication_dates` (calendar, pair), `cited_works.year_calendar` |
| **Error reports for Mandumah's catalogue**: invalid ISSNs, a stem on several records, duplicate 001s and authorities, wrong page ranges or years. Each has its evidence on the ink, a status, and a recheck against the next export | Unique to our position between print and catalogue | `catalogue_reports`, `catalogue_exports` |
| **The holes map**: for every issue, what is catalogued, what has a file, what is read, and what exists on paper but not in the index | Needs catalogue and bucket together | `publications.status`, `documents.level`, `v_issue_coverage` |
| **Semantic search that opens the page**: a Qdrant hit goes to its document and block, and its ink is one click away | Mandumah's search stops at the PDF | `vector_points`, `documents.qdrant_doc_id` |
| **Users' search feedback** as relevance judgments, used to tune search and to point at bad metadata | Private to Mandumah | `search_feedback` (columns fixed once the dump is inspected) |
| **Running heads and printed page numbers as evidence**: at 1M documents, journal, issue and page range can be checked from Azure alone, with no model | Others ignore the margins | `page_regions`, `pages.printed_folio`, `pages.running_head` |
| **Figures as data**: table cells, LaTeX and MathML, descriptions in Arabic and English, text inside pictures, all searchable | PMC has some of this. No Arabic index has it | `figures`, `table_cells`, `search_units` |
| **Reproducible and costed**: every article version records its run, prompt, rules hashes, model, cost and load | Others are opaque | `runs`, `versions`, `model_calls`, `load_events`, `patch_applications` |

**What stays in files, and why.** These are held as keys under a `storage_root` and never copied into the db:

- **Scans, page images, crops and the ink PDFs.** Binary data. Crops are rendered on demand from the anchor box.
- **Azure JSON and ALTO words.** About 4 billion words at 1M documents. The db keeps anchors, lines (for structured
  documents only) and roll-ups.
- **JATS trees.** The db holds what it needs to query, and the masters remain the source.
- **Raw judge answers and patch files.** Kept as file, sha and parsed columns. A raw copy goes into the db only for
  person patches, because those cannot be regenerated.
- **Qdrant vectors.** The db maps points to documents and blocks.
- **The MySQL feedback dumps.** Loaded as derived judgments, without user identities.
- **HTML views and legacy PDFs.** The db records only that they exist.

## 3. How the five audits' conflicts were resolved

| Question | Audits disagreed | Decision and reason |
|---|---|---|
| What is "an article"? | Bibliographic: publications, documents and versions. The others: everything keyed by stem | **Three levels.** `publications` holds the scholarly item (`W`+999$c, or `WS`+stem when uncatalogued). `documents` holds the file (a thesis has about 10). `versions` holds one run's reading of one file. Content and quality rows are keyed by the integer `version_id`, which is short and partitions cleanly at scale. |
| What is the stable "where"? | Content: Azure word spans, run-independent. Quality: an anchor row per version | **Azure word span** (`<stem>|p<page>|w<first>-<last>`). The Azure JSON never changes, while ALTO line and word ids moved in 14 of 299 articles. The quality audit's `method` column is kept: words, line, box or page. |
| Which line ids? | `ink_lines` (stem, run) vs `lines` (version, label) | **Two tables.** `azure_lines` is Azure's own lines, the same for every run. `line_map` is per version and maps ALTO line id ↔ Azure line ↔ block or region. A flag given as "4:2" lands on its block with one join. |
| Corrections ledger | `readings` vs `text_corrections` | One table, **`text_corrections`**, with layer `azure-misread` or `misprint`, `applied_to_text`, Azure's reading and confidence, the printed form, the proposed form, an actor and a status. |
| D33 changes | A `metadata_changes` table vs `metadata_assertions` | **`metadata_assertions` only.** A correction is an assertion that `supersedes` the printed one. `v_metadata_changes` is the old log. Catalogue disagreements become a live view. |
| Catalogue reports | Three different proposals | **One `catalogue_reports`** covering every kind (ISSN, records, authorities, names, pages, years…), with `who_is_right`, an anchor, status, batch and the export where it was found and fixed. |
| Patches | `entity_patches` (people) vs `patches` (structure) | **One `patches`** table with `domain` (content, metadata, entity, citation, catalogue) and `priority`, keyed `run|stem|patch_id`. This also cures the journal-code id collision that lost j-1677-p1-1. What a build did with each patch goes in `patch_applications`. |
| Human checks | `link_reviews` vs `reviews` | **One `reviews`** table for any subject, with a fingerprint so a verdict re-attaches after a rebuild or is marked stale. It also loads the judge verdicts, the judge truth file, gold and handcheck. |
| Ids that survive rebuilds | Registry vs natural keys | **Natural keys where they exist**: `W`+c999, `J`+code, stem, Azure anchors and mention ids. **An append-only `id_registry`** for A (person), I (institution) and C (cited work), whose natural keys are an authority id, a corporate authority id or a founding mention. Retired ids go in **`merged_ids`**. |
| Citation link target | A stem or a c999 | **`target_pub_id`**. Publications include catalogued-only items, so a link to a catalogue record and a link to a scanned article are the same kind of link, and a link survives when a catalogued item is later scanned. External targets (DOI, ISBN, Wikidata) use `target_kind = 'external'`. |
| Stable mention id | Hash of the printed span, or ALTO line ids | **Azure word span plus a sequence number.** It survives a reread, a fix of the text, and ALTO renumbering. |
| Authorship per run or per publication | People: per run | **Mentions per version, authorships per publication.** An authorship is built from the shown version plus the catalogue plus patches, and records `from_version`. Comparing runs means comparing mentions. |
| Keep all versions' content? | Content: current only. Quality: all | **All versions now** (four runs × 300 articles is small). At scale, keep the shown version plus any exam version. Older versions are retired to files with `content_in_db = 0`, and the `versions` row and its hashes stay. |
| Search | FTS in SQLite vs a separate engine | **Both, by level.** `search_units` and `search_fts` hold folded and clitic-stripped text for structured documents, updated per version with no corpus-wide rebuild. Azure-only full text for 1M documents goes in a search engine fed from S3 (deferred). |

Kept from v46 because it works: a rebuild from sources reproduces every table; raw and resolved values sit side by
side; flag ids are natural keys stable across runs; the loader is idempotent; and every table has a plain
description in Datasette (the `@role` lines carry this forward).

## 4. What we take from the big databases, and where we differ

- **OpenAlex.** We take the authorship as a link object with raw strings beside resolved ids
  (`byline_mentions` → `authorships`, `affiliation_mentions` → `affiliation_links`), type-prefixed ids,
  `merged_ids`, and dehydrated embeds in exports. **We differ** in keeping unmatched citations: OpenAlex drops them.
- **Web of Science.** We take the cited-reference index (`cited_works` exists even with no target), variants grouped
  under one work (`cited_work_members`, which a person can move), and self-citation shown with and without. Our
  authority ids play the role of claimed ResearcherIDs.
- **Crossref.** We take two-stage matching (full-text candidates, then validation, with thresholds tuned on
  `reviews`), `asserted_by` on every link, and typed relations with an inverse (`work_relations`).
- **ROR and WoS Organization-Enhanced.** We take institutions with a parent, names typed per language with no single
  "primary" name, and printed affiliations kept and searchable apart from the unified institution.
- **CSL position rules.** We take ibid. = the previous citation and op. cit. = the earliest full citation by the same
  author. We can apply them because we have the note stream in reading order.
- **ISSN-L.** Print and online ISSNs are grouped, and each one is check-digit tested (`journal_issns`).

## 5. Citations, worked through

An article's notes, in reading order:

```
(3)  ابن منظور، لسان العرب، دار صادر، بيروت، ج ٢، ص ٤٥.      -> mention m1  kind=full   full=m1
(4)  المرجع نفسه، ص ٤٧.                                     -> mention m2  kind=ibid   full=m1  method=previous-mention
(9)  الجاحظ، البيان والتبيين، ج ١، ص ١٢.                     -> mention m3  kind=full   full=m3
(11) ابن منظور، المرجع السابق، ج ٣، ص ١٠.                   -> mention m4  kind=op_cit full=m1  method=same-author-earliest
(12) المرجع السابق، ص ١١.                                    -> mention m5  kind=ibid   full=m1   (chain m5->m4->m1 collapsed)
```

- `citation_mentions` gets five rows. Each has its locator (volume and page cited), its ink anchor, the
  correction and flag counts on its lines, and a trust score.
- `cited_work_members` puts m1 into cited work C… «لسان العرب». Every other article's full citation of
  «لسان العرب» joins the same card (author key + title key, then a person can merge or split).
- `v_times_cited` for that card: citing publications = distinct articles (the WoS headline); mentions = m1, m2, m4
  and m5 here (4), plus every other article's mentions.
- `citation_links`: the card has no row, because Mandumah does not hold the book. If a review of it is in the
  catalogue, that is a `work_relations` row (`is_reviewed_in` → publication W…), not a citation link.
- Self-citation: `v_author_self_citations` matches persons. The citing article's `authorships.person_id` must equal
  the cited work's `cited_work_authors.person_id`, and both persons come from authority ids or reviewed clusters. A
  surname match alone (which gave false hits on «محمد») is never enough.
- Quran: «سورة البقرة، الآية ٢٥٥» in a note is a mention of kind `quran` plus a `quran_citations` row. It is left out
  of book times-cited and counted in `v_quran_index` instead.
- An unresolved back-reference keeps `full_mention_pk` NULL with `unresolved_why`. It is never guessed silently.

## 6. Provenance and trust on every value

- **Who said so.** Content comes from its `version_id` (→ run → prompt, rules hashes, model, cost). Metadata comes
  from `metadata_assertions.form`, `source` and `actor_id`. Links, authorships and memberships carry `method`,
  `score` (NULL when nothing was compared), `asserted_by` and `evidence`. Every row traces to a `load_id`.
- **Where it came from.** Each value has an `anchor_pk` on its row, with more anchors in `anchor_links` if needed.
  The anchor's `method` says how precise it is.
- **What changed it.** `patches` → `patch_applications`. D33 uses `supersedes` chains. `text_corrections` has a
  status that reviews change.
- **How far to trust it.** A trust score is derived from the evidence. The starting formula is in `v_block_trust`;
  mentions inherit from their lines. Each release recalibrates it against `reviews` (gold and hand checks) and
  `v_judge_precision`. The weights live in the view, which is versioned with the schema, so a trust number can always
  be explained.
- **Which value is shown.** For metadata the order is person > judge > D33-corrected > printed > catalogue >
  gemini-title > Qdrant payload, written into `chosen_rule`. For versions the order is person > complete >
  purpose (final > judged > base > exam > sample) > newest, written into `current_reason`. v33_300b therefore loads
  as an exam version beside v33_300_final, and final stays shown.

## 7. Scale to 1M documents

**Decision: SQLite stays for now, as both builder and server (Datasette), with a PostgreSQL path written in.** The
schema uses only portable types, JSON as text and partial indexes. Two things are SQLite-specific: `search_fts`
(FTS5), and scalar `max()` in `v_block_trust`, which PostgreSQL spells `greatest()`.

Switch to PostgreSQL, partitioned by `version_id` ranges or by journal, when any one of these becomes true:

- the structured layer passes about 50k articles or about 20 GB;
- people edit through the platform (concurrent writers for patches and reviews);
- the platform is served publicly.

That is a turning point for the owner, not a default.

**Levels.** Not every document gets every table:

| Level | Documents | Rows in the db |
|---|---|---|
| catalogued-only | about 87k articles, and 1.56M records in total | `publications`, `catalogue_*`, assertions |
| legacy-pdf | 308,593 | `documents` |
| azure | 1,013,912 | `documents`, `pages` (sizes, Azure confidence, folio and running head from Azure), metadata assertions from the catalogue, Gemini title files and Qdrant payloads, `vector_points`; full text in a search engine |
| structured / judged / final | read by Gemini runs | everything: versions, blocks, lines, anchors, mentions, corrections, quality |

What makes it scale:

- **Integer keys.** `version_id`, `mention_pk` and `anchor_pk` replace the composite TEXT keys v46 used everywhere.
- **Per-version loads.** Inserting or replacing one version touches only its rows and its search rows. People,
  institutions and journals are re-derived only for the keys a load touched. A full rebuild is a separate explicit
  command.
- **Word-level data stays in ALTO and Azure files.** At 1M documents, ALTO holds about 4 billion words.
- **Lines stay out of the db for Azure-only documents.** About 540M lines would otherwise be loaded.
- **Heavy views are materialised per release** (`_m` tables carrying the release `load_id`, the window and the
  document types), the way OpenAlex snapshots carry `updated_date` and `merged_ids`.
- **Rough size.** About 175 GB per fully structured version at 1M documents (the quality audit's 14.7 KB a page). This
  is why only the shown version and exams keep content in the db.

### Provenance and identity — where every row came from, and ids that never break

**`storage_roots`** — A place files live (local disk, an S3 bucket), so the database stores keys relative to it, never absolute paths.  
PK (root_id)  
Columns: `root_id` (e.g. 'local-43', 's3-source-docs', 's3-legacy'), `kind` (local | s3), `base` (path or s3://bucket/prefix in this environment), `note`

**`runs`** — One processing run (a Gemini/Argon reading, a judge pass, a final rebuild, an exam): what it ran with and what it cost.  
PK (run)  
FK root_id → storage_roots(root_id); parent_run → runs(run)  
Columns: `run` (v2_55, v33_300, v33_300_judged, v33_300_final, v33_300b), `kind` (sample | base | judged | final | exam | replay), `parent_run` (the run whose readings this one builds on), `model`, `thinking`, `mode`, `prompt_hash`, `rules_hash`, `rules43_hash`, `masters43_hash`, `evidence43_hash`, `judge_prompt_hash`, `code_commit`, `started`, `finished`, `usd`, `tokens_in`, `tokens_out`, `pages`, `root_id`, `root_rel` (the run folder), `note`

**`load_events`** — One execution of the loader or linker, so every row can be traced to the load that wrote it.  
PK (load_id)  
FK export_id → catalogue_exports(export_id); run → runs(run)  
Columns: `load_id`, `started`, `finished`, `tool` (load47 | link47 | people47 | report47 | rebuild47), `tool_hash`, `run`, `export_id`, `args`, `n_loaded`, `n_unchanged`, `rows_by_table` (JSON)

**`model_calls`** — Every paid model call ever made for a run (from gemini_spend.jsonl, raw chunk files, judge answers), so the cost of any page, article or verdict adds up.  
PK (call_pk)  
FK root_id → storage_roots(root_id); run → runs(run)  
Columns: `call_pk`, `called`, `model`, `job` (job: read | retry | judge | title | numbers ...), `run`, `stem`, `page`, `chunk`, `mode`, `tokens_in`, `tokens_out`, `tokens_think`, `usd`, `finish`, `root_id`, `file_key`, `sha`

**`actors`** — Who (which person, agent, model or code rule) made an assertion, a patch or a review.  
PK (actor_id)  
Columns: `actor_id` ('person:owner', 'agent:link47', 'model:gemini-3.8-flash', 'rule:d33-pages'), `kind` (person | agent | model | rule), `name`, `note`

**`id_registry`** — The append-only register of public ids (A persons, I institutions, C cited works), so a rebuild gives every entity the same id again.  
PK (public_id); unique (kind, natural_key)  
FK created_load → load_events(load_id)  
Columns: `public_id` ('A000123', 'I000045', 'C0001234'), `kind` (person | institution | cited_work), `natural_key` ('auth:111978' | 'mention:<mention_id>' | 'founder:<mention_id>' | 'cat-corp:2631'), `created_load`

**`merged_ids`** — When two ids turn out to be one thing (or one splits), the retired id points to the survivor so no link or bookmark breaks.  
PK (old_id)  
FK patch_key → patches(patch_key)  
Columns: `old_id`, `new_id`, `kind` (publication | person | institution | cited_work | journal), `relation` (merged-into | split-from), `merged`, `reason`, `asserted_by` (person | judge | link47 | catalogue), `patch_key`

### The ink layer — the scan and Azure's reading, fixed per document and the same for every run

**`documents`** — One file in the archive (an article PDF or one part of a thesis): where it is and how far it has been processed.  
PK (stem)  
FK first_load → load_events(load_id); root_id → storage_roots(root_id); pub_id → publications(pub_id)  
Columns: `stem` (S3 id, e.g. 0005-012-004-007 or 9802-001-009-0299-R), `pub_id`, `part_label` (thesis part suffix: T A C F 1..n O R), `part_kind` (article | title-page | abstract | contents | chapter | conclusion | references), `part_seq`, `level` (legacy-pdf | azure | structured | judged | final | person-checked), `in_main_bucket`, `legacy_only`, `has_azure_json`, `has_html`, `has_gemini_title`, `scan_pages`, `pdf_sha`, `azure_sha`, `root_id`, `scan_key`, `azure_key`, `html_key`, `gemini_title_key`, `scan_pdf_key` (scan_pdf = inkscript ink PDF), `qdrant_doc_id` (= stem in mandumah_v2 payloads), `first_load`

**`pages`** — One scanned page: its size, image, printed folio and running head, and Azure's own confidence on it.  
PK (stem, page)  
FK stem → documents(stem)  
Columns: `stem`, `page` (scan page, 1-based), `width_px`, `height_px` (ALTO page size (the box convention)), `image_w`, `image_h`, `dpi`, `page_image_key`, `printed_folio`, `printed_folio_int` (from the role.pageNumber region (evidence for page ranges)), `running_head` (from role.pageHeader (evidence for journal / issue)), `n_words`, `wc_mean`, `wc_min`, `n_low_wc` (Azure word confidences, run-independent), `angle`

**`azure_lines`** — One line as Azure read it (its "P:N" label), with its box and the span of Azure word numbers on it; kept only for structured documents.  
PK (stem, page, line_no)  
FK stem, page → pages(stem, page)  
Columns: `stem`, `page`, `line_no` (N in the 'P:N' labels of flags, patches, judge targets and fixes), `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, `first_word`, `last_word` (Azure word ordinals on the page), `n_words`, `wc_min`, `text` (Azure's reading of the line)

**`ink_anchors`** — An exact place on the scan (a page and a span of Azure words, or a box) that any value can cite as its evidence: "show me the ink".  
PK (anchor_pk); unique (anchor_key)  
FK stem, page → pages(stem, page)  
Columns: `anchor_pk`, `anchor_key` ('<stem>|p<page>|w<first>-<last>' (stable across runs) or '<stem>|p<page>|b<x>,<y>,<w>,<h>'), `stem`, `page`, `first_word`, `last_word`, `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, `text_azure`, `wc_min`, `method` (words | line | box | page  (how precisely the value was placed))

**`anchor_links`** — Extra evidence places for a value that needs more than one (a citation running across two pages, a name printed twice).  
PK (anchor_pk, owner_kind, owner_key, role)  
FK anchor_pk → ink_anchors(anchor_pk)  
Columns: `anchor_pk`, `owner_kind` (citation_mention | byline_mention | assertion | correction | flag | finding | patch | quran | figure ...), `owner_key`, `role`, `seq`

### The reading layer — one transparent sheet per run laid on the ink: text, structure, corrections

## 10. Deferred, and the questions for the owner

Deferred, with where each one stops today:

- **Search over the 1M Azure-only documents** in an engine (OpenSearch, or PostgreSQL full-text search with Arabic
  folding) fed from S3. Today `pages`, `documents` and `vector_points` only.
- **`search_feedback` columns.** They will be fixed after one dump is inspected locally (6.3 GB, in S3). Users are
  stored only as a salted hash.
- **`vector_points` block mapping.** It depends on whether mandumah_v2 points carry offsets into Azure's content.
  Until then the mapping is doc-only.
- **Hadith index.** Mentions of kind `hadith` exist, but there is no reference collection yet.
- **Quran reference text.** The choice is Tanzil Uthmani or simple, and the licence must be checked for a commercial
  platform before `quran_ayat` is loaded.
- **External ids.** Wikidata, VIAF, ROR and ISBN columns exist, and nothing fills them yet.
- **Topics/fields classifier**, CiteScore-style journal metrics and h-index. These are views per release once the
  linker is tuned.
- **Upstream fixes in experiment 43, outside this scope.** The masters should use full-stem patch ids, emit the Azure
  line label on every ALTO TextLine, and emit word ids for fixes and misprints. v2 works around all three:
  `patch_key` includes the stem, and `line_map` and anchors are built by box overlap.

Questions at this turning point. Nothing is decided on the owner's behalf:

1. **SQLite now, PostgreSQL later, triggered by the conditions in §7.** Is that acceptable, and where will the public
   platform live?
2. **`W`+999$c as the publication id**, with `WS`+stem for the 13,680 uncatalogued files, merged later?
3. **Thesis parts** (the «المصادر والمراجع» PDF, chapters): are they pages of one thesis publication (proposed:
   documents of one publication, each part a citation source), or separate searchable items?
4. **Partial v2_55 articles** (24 of 54 have 5 pages or fewer): show them marked partial (proposed), or hide them?
5. **When the first mention is a short form, which counts as "full"?** Proposed: the earliest mention with the most
   fields. **Times cited**: count citing articles as the headline and show mentions beside it (proposed)?
6. **Quran and hadith**: keep them out of book times-cited and count them in their own index (proposed)?
7. **Classical authors printed as the byline of an edited text** (e.g. «ابن القيم»): role `original-author`, with
   the محقق as `editor-of-text` (proposed)?
8. **Catalogue duplicate authorities** (111978/111979): one person for us, with a report sent to Mandumah
   (proposed)? Who sends reports, per batch or continuously, and who records "fixed"?
9. **The academic family tree** (`supervisions`): in v2's first build, or later?
10. **Public person pages**: should emails and titles (د., أ.د.) be shown?
