Ordinary OCR guesses which letters a picture shows and throws the picture away. This keeps the picture: every mark on the page becomes a shape, the shapes become a font, and the font is placed back exactly where the ink was. Copying gives the words; zooming never blurs; the highlight sits on the letters themselves.
